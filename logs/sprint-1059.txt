## Explicitly NOT in this sprint

- **Any maths change.** Changing a threshold, weight, `slPct` or `tpPct` requires a **copied
  configuration** and a monitored comparison (`maths-change-requires-ab`) ÔÇö and #916 is the reason
  that rule exists. This sprint **names the mechanism**; whether the behaviour is intended is a
  separate decision.
- **Entry-side work** ÔÇö `minComposite`, protocol additions, signal rescaling. Stated because the
  backlog is heavily skewed that way and it is not where the loss is.
- **#915** (signal strength capped at 11ÔÇô44%) and **#956** (scoring symbols that cannot trade) ÔÇö
  both real, both entry-side, both large.

## Definition of done

- The actor closing winners is **named**, with the `closeReason`/`stop_adjustment`/log evidence that
  identifies it ÔÇö or the residual uncertainty is stated with the row counts that bound it.
- Realised payoff is reported **per bot beside win rate**, and a bot whose payoff makes it
  unprofitable cannot be promoted by a high win rate (**#916 item 3**, which is a rating defect).
- Every claim carries its command and output. `honest-partial` is a pass; a ticked box that was not
  observed is not.
- One handoff, one register row per settled decision, guards canaried **both directions**.
