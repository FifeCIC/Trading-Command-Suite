# Sprint ÔÇö Fault containment: bridges that fail as a group, and signals that die before dispatch

> **Owner:** the second agent (the owner will name the issue to work through).
> **Label:** `sprint`. One per agent per week.
> **Theme (owner, 2026-10-01):** *"bot handling of existing positions, exits, or faults ÔÇö anything
> that adds strength to trading."*

---

## Why this is a sprint, in one measurement

Two of the seven live mechanisms that can stop this system trading are **faults with no containment**,
and two more are **signals that are produced and then die silently at 100%**.

| The fault | Measured |
|---|---|
| A reconnect on ONE bridge can drop **every** bridge on the terminal | both live bridges fell to `terminal-down` and recovered only when `/reconnect` was driven on **each, by hand** ÔÇö while **two watchdogs POST it unattended** (#1036) |
| Signals scored on a ticker no bot trades | `SPY` scored **37├ù/day**; its only bot trades **US500** ÔåÆ **0 executed** (#970) |
| Valid signals refused because a quote is missing | VIX **23/day** refused, GER40 **15/day** refused ÔåÆ **0 executed**, 100% loss (#972) |
| The daily bar series is corrupted by a repeated Friday bar | **166** of 205 duplicates are Friday, on a series with **no Friday session** (#1049) |

**Every one of these is silent.** Nothing errors; the signal is scored, or the bridge is "connected",
and the output looks like a normal run. That is this repo's signature defect, and it is why these
four belong together.

## Explicitly NOT in this sprint

- **Anything on the exit path** ÔÇö stops, trailing, the guardian, time-stop, `closeReason`. That is the
  **other** sprint (#916, #917, #998, #1041, #1056). Keeping the two apart is deliberate: they touch
  different files, so two agents cannot collide.
- **Any maths change** ÔÇö a threshold, weight or sizing value needs a copied configuration
  (`maths-change-requires-ab`).
- **#898** (architecture data duplication) and **#1042** (no reference-data layer) ÔÇö real, large, not
  trading-behaviour, and not this week.

## Definition of done

- The fault is **reproducible by a stated command**, and the fix is shown to remove it ÔÇö or the
  residual is stated with the measurement that bounds it.
- **A guard canaried BOTH directions.** A guard never observed to fail is a decoration; several of
  these are silent-failure classes, so a symptom guard beats a cause guard.
- Every claim carries its command and output. `honest-partial` is a pass; a ticked box that was not
  observed is not.
- ÔÜá´©Å **A bridge may be taken down to reproduce #1036** ÔÇö the stack is demo, recovery is
  `scripts/tcs-launch.ps1`, and the owner has said dev-induced downtime needs no report. **Say so in
  one line when you do it**, so the other agent is not surprised by a dead bridge mid-run.
