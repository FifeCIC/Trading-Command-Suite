# [Sprint] A/B measurement ÔÇö run it through BACKTESTING, and make a verdict refuse to speak on a thin sample

> or #962.

---

## Why this sprint exists ÔÇö an attempt to make A/B *runnable* found three blockers

#929 was Tier 0 of **#960** and was assumed to be a small unlock ("no bot has a second version").
It was not. Three separate faults, each found by measurement:

1. **The trading mode came from a SCORING field.** `snapshot.mode` holds `'score'` and was compared
   against the trading mode `'auto'`. Measured on the live DB: `scoring_strategy.mode` is
   `score`├ù18 / `semi-auto`├ù7 / `manual`├ù2 and **`auto` ├ù0** ÔÇö so `mode !== 'auto'` was true for
   every stack that has ever existed and **the order branch was unreachable**. Fixed.
2. **The cadence was a literal `'5m'`** in a system whose canonical resolver is
   `selectBaseTimeframe()`. Measured, same protocol, same second: 15m bars ÔåÆ `94/BUY`; 5m bars ÔåÆ
   `15/HOLD`. The runner was measuring a configuration that does not exist. Fixed.
3. **ÔÜá´©Å THE ACCOUNT NETS.** Two bots on one symbol merge into a SINGLE broker position. Measured:
   three XAUUSD orders became one position of `vol 0.04` (bot `0.02` + side A `0.01` + side B
   `0.01`) under the last-written comment; both side rows were then left `exit-unknown` with
   `pnl = NULL`, 48 seconds after opening.

**And the owner identified the consequence:** *"most A/B protocols will score similar at least if the
changes are minor, which means two signals for trading at the same time."*

**A/B exists to test a MINOR change. So the two sides signal together almost every time.** The live
pair therefore **cannot** be the measuring instrument ÔÇö it keeps its LOCK (#963) and becomes the
**final confirmation step**. The measurement is the **backtest**, where there is no broker, no merge
and no collision, and both sides are scored over the **identical** bars.

---

## Order of work ÔÇö do not reorder

### 1. THE SAMPLE BAR ÔÇö do this FIRST, before any new entry point

**Evidence (measured 2026-09-23):**

```
xau-speed-m15  totalTrades: 3   winRate: 100   sharpeRatio: 42.31
xau-speed-m5   totalTrades: 0   winRate: 0
best: { id: <m15>, metric: "sharpeRatio", value: 42.31 }     <- POST /api/backtest/runs/compare
```

**The comparison named a winner on THREE trades**, presented identically to a result from three
hundred. This is the exact defect `AB_MIN_TRADES_FOR_VERDICT` was added for in the snapshot trial
(*"noise wearing a result's clothes"*) ÔÇö **and the backtest comparison has no such bar.**

**A winner named on three trades is worse than no winner, because it gets acted on.** Every entry
point below feeds this verdict, so building them first multiplies the problem.

- **Deliverable:** `runs/compare` refuses to name a `best` below a stated per-side trade count, and
  says `insufficient-data` **with the counts** when it does.
- **Guard:** a spec proving both directions, canaried ÔÇö plant a 3-trade sample, confirm no winner;
  raise it above the bar, confirm a winner *with its method named*.

### 2. `compare` disagrees with the run it compares

Same run, two stories: `run` reported `netPnl ÔêÆ0.0009`; `compare` shows `totalPnl: 0` and
`maxDrawdown: 27` (vs the run's `maxTradeDrawdown ÔêÆ0.0003`). **Find which is right before building on
either** ÔÇö the compare view is reading different fields, and a viewer that disagrees with its source
cannot be trusted as a second source.

### 3. Backtest a STRATEGY (bot) ÔÇö not currently expressible

`BacktestRunRequest` takes `symbol / days / timeframe / protocolId / slPct / tpPct / ÔÇª` ÔÇö **no bot id**.
Needs a `botId` that resolves to protocol + `slPct`/`tpPct` + sizing, so *"backtest this bot"* is one
call rather than a hand-assembled request.

ÔÜá´©Å Read **#843** first ÔÇö the backtester models position sizing **4 ways** and the live path sizes it
**1 way**. A strategy backtest that sizes differently from the live bot is measuring a different bot.

### 4. Test a PAIR ÔÇö the pieces exist, they are just not joined

`POST /runs/compare` genuinely works (verified: both runs side by side, plus a `best`). And a pair
**already materialises its change as a second protocol** ÔÇö `duplicate()` copies the source protocol
and records `Protocol.derivedFrom`, so the two sides are already two distinct `protocolId`s that
`POST /run` already accepts. **This is probably wiring, not a new override mechanism** ÔÇö but do not
assume it; run both protocols of a real pair and compare.

### 5. Then the surfaces ÔÇö #819, then #606

- **#819 ÔÇö the replay view.** *"a visually generated list, with speed control so that I can watch
  trade actions as they happen"*, and the owner has already said *"I think you should build this
  now."* **Nothing new is needed here; it is outstanding.**
- **#606 ÔÇö Chart Indicator System** ÔÇö library-agnostic indicator rendering. **ADR-005 chose
  Lightweight Charts over the TradingView widget**; this is not an open 2ÔÇô3 way question.

### 6. Then the rename ÔÇö #962

`ab_test` / `AbTestService` / the `TCS AB-A`/`AB-B` broker tags, out of "A/B". **87 references across
30 files**, and **two different things share the substring** (`/lab/ab-testing` is the protocol
comparison page; `ab_test` is the snapshot trial), plus ~22 historical doc references that must
**not** be rewritten. Scoped in the issue with the step list.

## What NOT to do

- **Do NOT create another sprint or epic.** #816 is the epic, #823 is the workstream, #819 and #606
  are the surfaces. A fourth surface is the churn this repo keeps paying for.
- **Do NOT build a live-pair measurement.** It was measured and it cannot work (blocker 3).
- **Do NOT build the two mechanisms the owner deferred.** Recorded on #963, explicitly not requested:
  **(a)** divergence *recording* (*"if A signalled for entry many minutes before B ÔÇª then that needs
  to be recognised"*), and **(b)** first-mover ownership between paired bots (*"the blocker then owns
  the additional profit or loss from that point onwards"* ÔÇö owner's own words: *"That is complex."*).
- **Do NOT move #929 / #963 back into #960.** They are A/B *measurement*; #960 is strategy
  establishment and is being advanced separately.
