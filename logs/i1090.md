## Measured 2026-10-08 — the "60 / 100" score is not a percentage, and not a second scoring path

Raised from the Page Walkthrough on two views (`/status/meta-stack-status`, `/status/meta-stack-explorer`),
both showing `60 / 100`. Owner: *"I need to know if the app is still generating a score using an approach
outside of the primary (and meant to be ONLY) META.Stack scoring path."*

**Answer: no second scoring path exists.** The score is the META.Stack composite. What is wrong is the
**denominator it is displayed against** — it is reported as a flat `100` regardless of the protocol.

### Evidence

```
GET /api/scoring/gated/protocols      → xau-speed-v1 "Gold Speed Scalp V1"
    maxScore: 1010            (11 directives; Σ directive maxScores = 1010 — consistent)

GET /api/scoring/ranked?limit=20      → XAUUSD row
    score:             106
    normalizedScore:   13.96226
    maxPossibleScore:  100        ← WRONG
    coverage: 1   degraded: false
```

Three different scales in one payload:

| Scale | Value | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| protocol capacity | **1010** | Σ directive `maxScore` — the protocol's own declared maximum |
| raw composite | **106** | what `/api/scoring/ranked` calls `score` (106 of 1010 = **10.5%**) |
| normalised | **13.96%** | implies a base of **≈759**, which is neither 1010 nor 100 |
| `maxPossibleScore` | **100** | hardcoded/flat — matches nothing |

### Why the UI misleads

`src/pages/MetaStackStatusPage.jsx:298-299`

```jsx
<div className="mss-priority-score-value">{priorityResult.score}</div>
<div className="mss-priority-score-label">Score / {priorityResult.maxPossibleScore || 100}</div>
```

So the page renders `106 / 100`, and on the day the owner read it, `60 / 100`. **That reads as a
percentage, and it is not one.** A composite of 60 against a protocol capacity of 1010 is **5.9% of
capacity** — a very different statement. The `|| 100` also means a *missing* field silently renders
as a denominator of 100, so the label cannot be trusted even as a fallback.

### ⚠️ Documentation contradicts the measurement

`src/pages/MetaStackExplorerPage.jsx:180` states the composite is *"clamped 0–100"*.
**The measured value is 106.** Either the clamp does not exist on this path, or the clamp is
applied elsewhere and `/api/scoring/ranked` returns a pre-clamp figure. The two cannot both be true,
and nothing errors either way — the repo's signature failure shape.

### Also documented, and relevant

- `MetaStackExplorerPage.jsx:159` — group score is *"sum(rawScores) ÷ sum(maxScores) × 100"*, i.e. a
  percentage over the protocol's own capacity.
- `MetaStackStatusPage.jsx:340` — the threshold checklist compares `score` (raw) against
  `scoringStrategy.entryThreshold || 60`, mixing a raw composite with a percentage-scale threshold.

### Not done here

- **The fix.** Display-only change is available and low risk (show `score / protocol.maxScore` and the
  real percentage), but the *backend* values (`maxPossibleScore`, and whether a clamp exists) are a
  contract question — three consumers read `score` and at least one read `normalizedScore`. Filed for
  a measured fix rather than a guessed one.
- **The score-mechanism UI** the owner asked for on `/trading/trading-bots` (task 7) — "merge a diagram
  with a dynamic page" — is a separate, larger piece of work.
