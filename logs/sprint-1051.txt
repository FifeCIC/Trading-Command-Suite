## R29.1 ÔÇö Instrument form: the direct answer first

**The owner asked:** *"a protocol can probably signal for both stocks and CFD ÔÇö if there is a
difference please tell me because it means we need different protocols for one symbol."*

### Answer: today there is **no difference on this platform**, and here is the measurement.

| Fact | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `symbol.type` holds an **asset class**, not a **trading form** | `stock` 213 ┬À `forex` 15 ┬À `etf` 13 ┬À `index` 9 ┬À `crypto` 7 ┬À `commodity` 3 (`symbol` table, 2026-10-01) |
| MT5 is the only live venue for those symbols | `symbol_route`: `mt5` 8 routes ┬À `binance` 4 routes |
| A "stock" on MT5 is a **CFD on the equity**, not the share | MT5 is a CFD/leveraged venue; there is no cash-equity book in this app |
| No options, futures or cash equities exist in the app | no instrument-form field exists anywhere |

**Consequence:** `stock`, `index`, `commodity`, `forex` all resolve to **one form** here ÔÇö a CFD
executed through MT5. So a single protocol **can** signal for a "stock" and a "CFD" without conflict,
because the app never trades the same underlying two different ways. **No separate protocols per
symbol are needed today.**

### But the owner's instinct is correct ÔÇö the hazard is LATENT, and the hooks are declared but dead

| Level | Field | State |
|---|---|---|
| **Directive** | `SuitabilityProfile.instrumentTypes` | **Declared in 118 places, branched on in ZERO.** A buried ability. `research-import.service.ts` even notes it *"would stop the FX"* use of `insider-activity` ÔÇö i.e. it is not wired. |
| **Protocol** | `SuitabilityService` compares `symbol.type` to `protocol.symbolTypes` | ÔÜá´©Å `protocol.symbolTypes` **is not a column**. It is heuristically *inferred* from slug/name/tradingStyle (`inferProtocolSymbolTypes()`). |
| **Bot** | (none) | `trading_strategy` has **no** instrument-class column at all. |

So the enforcement the owner wants ("warning/prevention to stop a CFD protocol's scores being
executed by an options bot") **has no working input today** ÔÇö the declarations exist and nothing
reads them.

### Proposed scope

1. **Wire `instrumentTypes` into the activation gate** (it exists; make it fire). A directive
   declaring `['stocks']` must not contribute to a symbol whose form it does not fit.
2. **Add an explicit `instrumentForm`** distinct from `symbol.type` ÔÇö values `cfd | spot | cash |
   future | option`. `symbol.type` stays the *underlying*; the form is *how it is traded*. This is
   the field that makes "stock vs CFD" answerable at all.
3. **Protocol declares the form(s) it is valid for** (a real column, replacing the inferred guess).
4. **Bot declares the form it trades**, and a protocolÔåöbot mismatch is a **warning at config time**
   and a **refusal at execution time**.
5. **CFD overnight awareness** ÔÇö see R29.3 below (overnight fees, holding past the close).

**Deferred (noted for preparation):** **options** get custom directives eventually. No option
handling is to be built now ÔÇö only the form field that makes room for them.

## R29.2 ÔÇö The exit process is *advanced*, not a literal new algorithm

> Owner: *"Caution on my 'new exit algorithm' expression ÔÇö there was already work done and I imagine
> it as an algorithm. Do not take my words literal. The exit process needs to be advanced, going
> through a series of checks, and many per-bot settings that make a strategy unique. It is my
> understanding risk management falls under the exit management part of bots."*

Existing exit machinery (do **not** rebuild ÔÇö advance it): `stop-guardian.service.ts` (the ONE
post-entry mechanism, #913), `exit-consensus.service.ts` (verdict over exit directives, #990/#1027),
`position-time-stop.service.ts` (max-hold sweep, #773), and the 5 exit directives
(`momentum-exhaustion`, `target-achieved`, `regime-shift-exit`, `time-stop`, `chandelier-exit`).

**Scope:** the exit path is a **chain of checks** with **per-bot settings**, and **risk management
is a member of that chain**, not a separate subsystem. Each stage must log its decision to the DB
and be visible on the bot detail view.

## R29.3 ÔÇö Hard ceilings, the override, and the overnight question

> Owner: *"The hard ceilings are the final hour of trading on the market the symbol belongs to
> UNLESS this is overridden by the user through selection of option, at which case they should also
> be asked if they will allow over-night holding (if CFD) on a position in profit only or do they
> wish to override for all positions? (Which should come with a strong risk warning, don't you
> think?)"*

Captured in full on **#1048**. This issue owns the **session-calendar** dependency it rests on:
"the final hour of the market the symbol belongs to" is only meaningful with **correct per-symbol
market/session timezones** ÔÇö the owner's words: *"this could go very wrong."*
